# EDA Memo

## 1. Data & Integrity

The corpus covers `34` campaign files, `C33` through `C66`, with `46,520` XAUUSD fill rows collapsed to `7,277` positions on `(campaignId, positionId)`; `5,224` of those positions had partial closes. The analysis separates the prelude era (`C33-C52`) from the primary era (`C53-C65`) because the product and participation mix change at `C53`, and it excludes `C41` and `C66` from statistics because each is a one-account test run. On the current primary-era memo sample, that leaves `6,582` positions across `13` campaigns, `496` active accounts, and `1,163` active `traderKey` values. Registrant files contained raw `email`, `ip_address`, and `telegram_username`; `sanitize_traders()` hashed usable emails to `traderKey`, assigned anonymous `ipClusterId` and `sharedIpFlag`, dropped the raw PII fields, and wrote only sanitized output columns.

## 2. Structural Findings

The gross-basis fade hurdle is deterministic: `reverseProfit = -profit - 7.00*amount` exactly, and CHECK 1 shows mean cost per lot of `7.0` for every campaign except `C41`, which is `6.5`. The state target for modeling is therefore `gross_loss_per_lot = -profit/amount`, with `7.00` as the gross hurdle. On the cost stack framing, net economics are commission-dominated: the memo decomposition is `-42.75 = -1.63 gross - 41.12 commission`. The current primary-era pipeline output is consistent with that direction: weighted `E[loss/lot]` is `3.998612` on a gross basis while weighted net cost is `41.994899` per lot. Median account lifespan is `1.988194` hours, reported in the pipeline as `1.99h`, so most behavior occurs inside a short session rather than across a long replay horizon.

The funnel changes materially at `C53`. Median active accounts per campaign move from `28.0` in the prelude era to `163.0` in the primary era, and mean active accounts per campaign move from `29.315789` to `162.846154`. Mean activation rate per campaign moves from `0.065280` in the prelude era to `0.340474` in the primary era. SL usage drops from `0.985570` in the prelude era to `0.761623` in the primary era, while mean `gross_loss_per_lot` flips from `-22.880694` in the prelude era to `12.436357` in the primary era. These discontinuities justify keeping `C53-C65` as the main modeling window and treating earlier campaigns as a separate context set rather than pooling them into one stationary sample.

## 3. Behavioral Hypotheses & Evidence

In the primary era, the baseline point estimate is above the gross hurdle: the MEMO NUMBERS block reports baseline `E[loss/lot] = 12.436357`, size-weighted `E[loss/lot] = 3.998612`, `n_positions = 6582`, `n_active_accounts = 496`, and `n_campaigns = 13`. The primary-era fade table ranks `loss_streak >= 2` first among the pre-registered triggers at `40.54` `gross_loss_per_lot` over `881` trades, with a trader-clustered `95%` bootstrap CI of `[11.36, 70.21]` and an IP-clustered CI of `[10.79, 68.00]`. That is the confirmed trigger. The exploratory interaction added after CHECK 9, `loss_streak>=2 AND small_size_flag`, is stronger in point estimate at `114.84` over `251` trades and remains above hurdle under both clusterings, with traderKey CI `[32.47, 198.33]` and `ipClusterId` CI `[31.53, 193.50]`.

The rest of the pre-registered condition set is directionally positive on the per-lot target in the primary era. Point estimates are `37.47` for `loss_streak >= 3`, `21.45` for `late + underwater`, `16.07` for `size escalation`, `12.37` for `late session`, `8.81` for `near rule-B cliff`, `8.49` for `fast re-entry after loss`, `7.66` for `same side after SL hit`, `7.17` for `no SL set`, and `2.24` for `deep drawdown`. On the clustered bootstrap, only `loss_streak >= 2` and the new `loss_streak>=2 AND small_size_flag` row clear the `7.00` hurdle at the `95%` lower bound; the other pre-registered rows remain positive in point estimate but not individually confirmed.

The no-SL row should be treated as a tail-harvesting effect rather than a stable central tendency effect. Within the primary era, `no SL set` has `1,569` trades and `E[loss/lot] = 7.17`, but the top `1%` of those trades, `16` positions, contribute `9,420.24` of `3,244.95` total `reverseProfit`, or `2.903046` times the total. The sign stays positive within era, but the concentration means the row is sensitive to a small number of outsized fades and should not be read as a broad average treatment effect.

CHECK 5 resolves the size question in two parts. First, equal-weighted and size-weighted economics diverge: `12.436357` `E[loss/lot]` collapses to `3.998612` when weighted by amount. Second, the linear rank relationship is not what carries the result: Spearman correlation between `amount` and `gross_loss_per_lot` is `-0.003596`, effectively flat, but the quartile table is not flat. By amount quartile, mean `gross_loss_per_lot` is `45.0323`, `-18.6198`, `12.9369`, and `1.2239`. Inside the confirmed trigger alone, the same quartiles become `114.8443`, `28.6911`, `10.0208`, and `-2.6801`. The relevant finding is therefore not a smooth monotone slope; it is that the edge is concentrated in the smallest-size bucket, including inside the one condition already supported by clustered inference.

## 4. Stage 2-3 Plan

Stage 2 should fit a composite model on `gross_loss_per_lot`, not on `reverseProfit`, with the current trigger set as causal state inputs and the `loss_streak>=2 AND small_size_flag` interaction persisted as an exploratory feature. Stage 3 evaluation should stay inside `C53-C65`, use walk-forward splits by campaign, and enforce `traderKey`-disjoint validation because `1,001` active traders appear in more than one campaign. The policy preview is a flat-size fade benchmark rather than a size-scaled policy: the gross edge is positive in equal-weighted units but compresses materially when weighted by amount, so any live sizing rule should wait until a size-conditioned model survives walk-forward and cross-trader validation.
